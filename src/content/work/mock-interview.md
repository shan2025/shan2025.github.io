## Why I built it

Placement interviews decide a lot for students. I wanted to give them realistic practice beforehand, so they walk into the real thing with more confidence, ready for both the technical and the HR round.

## What it does

- A curated bank of frequently asked **technical** and **HR** questions, inspired by the interviews at major tech companies.
- Practice set up like a real interview, not a list of questions to read.
- A simple, student-friendly interface, so nobody needs instructions.

It was built for my Bachelor of Computer Applications course, with PHP, Java, HTML and CSS, and Python for supporting scripts.

## What I'd add next

- An AI interviewer that listens to your answer and gives feedback.
- A dashboard that tracks how you improve across sessions.
- Questions that get harder or easier depending on how you're doing.
