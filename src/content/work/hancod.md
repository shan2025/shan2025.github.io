## Business Analyst Intern, March to May 2026

I wrote the documents engineers build from: functional specifications (FSDs), high-level design documents (HFDs) and functional requirement documents (FRDs) for more than 12 product modules. I turned UI and UX designs into functional specs and ran requirement walkthroughs with the team.

Every spec follows the same shape: the business goal in one sentence, user stories for each need, and acceptance criteria written **before** design starts, so "done" is agreed up front. I then led end-to-end testing, from writing test scenarios to tracking every defect to closure across design, engineering and QA.

## Growth Manager Trainee, since June 2026

Now I own user acquisition and conversion for a live service website. I shape product priorities from market and customer insight, and turn business goals into requirements the team can build.

## What carried over

A build that passes every test case can still miss what the business asked for, and a campaign can launch on time and still bring nobody. In both jobs the habit is the same: decide what "worked" means before building, then check the real result, not the status. I wrote about it in [Status is not the outcome](/writing/ran-is-not-worked/).
