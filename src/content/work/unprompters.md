---
name: Unprompters
kind: Solo product, in progress
order: 0
ran: A marketplace for work made by people — code, websites, writing, poetry, art, photography, design, music and ideas. Every listing carries an honest AI score, for example "3% AI, likely 2–13%, Human-made", so buyers can tell how much of a piece a human actually made.
worked: The score is built to be hard to game. Creators disclose where AI helped at four stages (idea, making, editing, extras) and the score never drops below what they admit; an automatic scan of the work can only raise it; process proof like drafts and edit history narrows the range; and people, not machines, settle disputes. It's still being built, with the honest-score engine, an Explore view with a "human-made only" filter, listing pages with passage-by-passage highlights and an embeddable badge, a guided selling flow with a "Check my score" step, and creator profiles already working.
